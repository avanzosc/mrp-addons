========================
MRP Product Configurator
========================

This module adds the standard Odoo product configurator to manufacturing
orders.

On a draft manufacturing order, the pencil button next to the product opens
the same configurator used on sales order lines. Confirming the configuration
updates the product variant on the manufacturing order. Odoo then recalculates
the bill of materials and its components.
