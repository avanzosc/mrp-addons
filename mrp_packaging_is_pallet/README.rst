=======================
MRP Packaging Is Pallet
=======================

This module extends the **Manufacturing Orders** form view in Odoo to filter packaging options based on whether they are **pallets** or **non-pallets**.  
It works as a **glue module** on top of `mrp_qty_by_packaging` and `product_packaging_is_pallet`.

- The field **`product_packaging_id`** only displays packagings marked as **pallets**.  
- The field **`packaging_id`** only displays packagings that are **not pallets**.
